Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Stemma (stemma.link) is a collaborative family tree editor run as a personal, non-commercial project by an individual operator ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data the service processes, why, and what rights you have over it. For any question about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights below, write to danilasergeevich@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Your Google account email address. You sign in with Google. We verify the identity token Google issues and keep only the email address it contains — it is your account identifier in Stemma. We do not receive or store your Google password, and we do not request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.
  • The family tree content you enter. Names, birth and death dates, free-text biographies, photographs, and the family relationships that connect people. Some of this is data about other people, living or deceased — see section 6.
  • Session data. A random session identifier, the time the session was created and last used, and the email it belongs to.
  • Technical logs. Our infrastructure providers record ordinary request logs — IP address, timestamp, requested path, user agent, response status — for security, debugging and abuse prevention.

We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party profiling of any kind, and we never sell or rent personal data.

2. Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To provide the service — authenticate you, store and display your trees, share them with people you invite. Legal basis: performance of a contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service secure and working — logs, rate limiting, abuse investigation, backups. Legal basis: our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To answer your emails when you contact us. Legal basis: legitimate interests.

3. Google sign-in and limited use

Authentication uses Google Identity Services. Stemma's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Concretely: the email address obtained through Google sign-in is used only to identify your account and to address invitations to you. It is not transferred to third parties except as described in section 5, not used for advertising, and not read by humans except where you ask us to for support, or where security or law requires it.

You can revoke Stemma's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Cookies

Stemma sets one cookie, stemma_session. It holds a random session identifier — no personal data is encoded in it. It is HttpOnly and Secure, it expires after 30 days, and it exists solely to keep you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service to function, so no consent banner is shown. We set no advertising or analytics cookies. Signing out deletes the session on our side; clearing your browser cookies removes it on yours.

5. Who else sees the data

  • People you invite. Invitation links you create grant the recipient access to the tree (and the subtree) you shared. Anyone holding a link you distribute can use it — treat invitation links as secrets.
  • Infrastructure providers (processors). Amazon Web Services hosts the application, the database and the photo storage in the eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany) region; Google provides sign-in. They process data on our instructions and are bound by their own data-processing terms.
  • AI clients you connect yourself. Stemma exposes a Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoint. If you add it as a connector in an AI assistant, that assistant can read and modify your trees on your behalf, and the content it reads is then subject to that vendor's own privacy policy. This connection only exists if you create it, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose data where we are legally obliged to.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Data about other people

A family tree is, by nature, information about other people. When you enter details about a living relative you are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so, and for telling them if they would reasonably expect to be told. If you are recorded in someone else's Stemma tree and want that removed, email us at danilasergeevich@gmail.com and we will act on it.

7. Retention

Tree content is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting a person, family or tree removes it — and any attached photos — from our storage. Sessions expire 30 days after they were created. Technical logs are kept for a short operational period (typically no more than 90 days) and then rotate out. Backups may retain deleted content for a limited period before being overwritten.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, export (portability), restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing rests on it. Most of this you can do directly in the app: edit or delete any person, family or tree, and export a tree from the menu. For anything else, email danilasergeevich@gmail.com — including a request to delete your account and everything in it. We aim to reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS only. Data is stored encrypted at rest by our providers. Access to production data is restricted to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not store information in Stemma that you could not tolerate being exposed.

10. Children

Stemma is not directed at children and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 16. Children may of course appear as people inside a tree, entered by an adult account holder under section 6.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
